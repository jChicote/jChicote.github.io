About

It's time to conquer your anxiety and order that overly complex coffee order for your superiors!

Be wary, however, as your orders get more and more complicated, you may start to stumble over your words. The baristas don't appear to be patient either...

How to play

  • You receive a card with all the drinks you need to order written on it
  • The barista asks how you want your coffee, step by step
  • Keep it simple: reply by typing the bare minimum, e.g. “Type?” → LATTE, “What size?” → EXTRA LARGE
  • Press Enter to submit your reply, but check it before answering
