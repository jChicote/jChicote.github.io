About

Universe Collide is a Battlefront-esque game of ship-to-ship battles between opposing teams or enemies. You play as any vessel, fighting against AI-driven opponents until you reign as the ace. You can play as any vessel from the Star Wars and Star Trek franchises, with future inclusions planned for vessels from Battlestar Galactica and some custom vessel designs.

Status

As a lone developer on the project, the current version is a demo with the crucial features in a loose free-for-all. Star Wars is currently the only franchise included, recreated as a virtual clone of the original game. With time, it should develop with more features.

Project purpose

The purpose of this game was primarily as a passion project to bring together different vessels into one game. It also serves as good practice in applying different areas of software design and principles to better construct the game's software architecture.

Approach

As a passion project, development started early with little design and project management. During the redevelopment into Unity 2019.3.4f, development has used the Kanban framework. No official timeline is set, as it is a continual process of open development and review.

Project progress

As of 4 November 2020

Progress has moved beyond the level of development initially reached before switching to the newer engine. The redevelopment addressed many of the issues seen in the initial development, such as handling multiple duplicates of values, through the flyweight pattern. It also flipped the order of development so the core mechanics and features are built before the artistic elements and asset creation.

The AI behaviours, contained in their own finite states, have mostly been converted and are more responsive than their previous iteration. The UI and scoring system have yet to be converted before developing other components to extend the concrete implementation.

Current features

  • Completed non-stylised main menu with a loading screen before heading into the main gameplay.
  • Extendable weapon components for quick and modifiable development of custom weapons.
  • Generalised design of base components, allowing for inherited instances of enemy AI, friendly AI, player objects and more.
  • Scriptable objects sharing constant values between multiple instances of objects, enabling lightweight designs.
  • Stat handling of core abilities and entity stats for centralised reading and writing of an entity's characteristics.
  • Dynamic switching of object states by exchanging components managed by the entity's state machine.
  • Rendering performed by the Universal Render Pipeline.
