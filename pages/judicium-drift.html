About

Today, all crimes, no matter how petty, are resolved by deadly chariot duel.

You are a lawyer. Your client's fate rides on your ability to out-drift the prosecution. Do you drive as well as you know the law?

Local multiplayer with two controllers (or a controller and keyboard) is great fun.

How to play

  • Drift to leave a trail
  • Form a complete loop to claim the area
  • Capture enemy drones and lawyers in your loop to kill them

Controls

  • Right triggerAccelerate
  • Left triggerReverse
  • Left joystickSteer
  • Bottom buttonDrift
  • Arrow keysAccelerate and steer
  • Space barDrift
